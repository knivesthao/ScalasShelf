## Key Qualitative Context from Research

**From the World Bank Lao Economic Monitor (June 2026):**
> "A third of households reduced spending on food, health and education"
> "Inflation rising again to near 10% in April, eroding household purchasing power and disproportionately affecting poor and vulnerable groups"
> "Limited spending on education, health, and social protection has undermined human capital"
> "Labor shortages are a critical constraint for private business" — many workers migrate to Thailand

**From UNICEF Lao PDR (Education section):**
> "Lao PDR still has some of the poorest education indicators in Southeast Asia"
> "High repetition and dropout rates in primary grades are strongly influenced by limited access to quality early childhood education"
> "Students' learning outcomes are low, leaving children without essential knowledge and skills"
> Key constraints include: "teachers' limited capacity, a weak pedagogical supporting system, challenges in multi-grade teaching, and **the lack of teaching-learning materials**"
