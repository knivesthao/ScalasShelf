## Path to Apply

The only viable route is through **Laos' National Commission for UNESCO**:

1. **Identify contact:** Find the Lao National Commission for UNESCO (likely under Ministry of Education and Sports)
2. **Build relationship:** Present Scala’s Shelf as a priority project aligned with Lao education goals
3. **Get prioritized:** The National Commission submits up to 7 requests — Scala’s Shelf needs to be in that list
4. **Wait for next cycle:** Next submission window targets ~early 2028 for the 2028–2029 biennium

> **Alternative (less likely):** If Scala’s Shelf's Lao partner company has NGO status or a relationship with an NGO in official UNESCO partnership, that NGO could submit via the NGO route (needs 2 Member States' support).
