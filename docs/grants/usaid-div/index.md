## Background

The DIV Fund is the independent successor to USAID's Development Innovation Ventures (DIV) program. When USAID was restructured, the DIV leadership team (Sasha Gallant, Michael Kremer, Jeff Brown) spun it out as a standalone nonprofit. Same model, same thesis, same team — now independent.

**Key people:**
- **Sasha Gallant** — Co-Founder & CEO (former USAID DIV Managing Director)
- **Michael Kremer** — Co-Founder & Board Chair (Nobel laureate, former DIV Scientific Director)
- **Jeff Brown** — Co-Founder & Chief Innovation Officer (former Evidence Action CEO, former GIF CEO)

## DIV Model

| Stage | Amount | What's Required |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| Stage 1 (Pilot) | Up to $200K | Concept note — prove the idea |
| Stage 2 (Test) | Up to $500K | Rigorous testing, demonstrated impact per dollar |
| Stage 3 (Scale) | Up to $1.5M | Proven cost-effective solution, path to scale |

We're targeting **Stage 1** — concept note submission.
