**schools**
users can be solo or in a school - all users have access to the full library regardless
*solo* 
default view of all searchable books. they will have to add books to their own reading list (let's call this a shelf or something)
*school*
curated subset relevant to the school
schools can be in different districts
schools can have grades with curated subset of school shelf (terminology, we're calling the playlist or list of books a shelf)
grades can have classes, an even farther curated subset of grade shelf
classes can have lesson plans, a personalized curated subset of class shelf tailored for the reader
