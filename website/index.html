The Problem

Books are a luxury in Laos.

A landlocked country of 7.5 million faces a literacy crisis driven by access, not ability. Physical books are expensive to import and nearly impossible to distribute across rugged, mountainous terrain. A third of households are cutting education spending, and the average child accesses fewer than one book a year outside textbooks.

Meanwhile, mobile phones reach over 80% of the population. The digital infrastructure exists. The content does not. Textweaver closes that gap by turning narration into downloadable stories that work on a $50 phone over a 2G connection.

The Solution

A content factory, not just a reader.

A creator writes a story — scene by scene, in Lao or English. AI reads the narration, Unreal Engine renders the visuals, and the digital library delivers a lightweight comic or interactive book a child can read offline.

How It Works

From narration to 3D story, automatically.

Textweaver is a pipeline of four orchestrated stages, each powered by a Unreal Engine capability that no WebGL approach could replicate — Blueprint-driven scene assembly, real-time rendering, Movie Render Queue, and Pixel Streaming for the browser-based creator tool.

Blueprint orchestrator

AI-generated assets are composed into 3D environments programmatically.

Comic post-process shader

Cel shading, edge detection, and halftone give every scene a consistent illustrated look.

Character consistency

IP-Adapter + ControlNet keeps a character identical from page 1 to page 20.
